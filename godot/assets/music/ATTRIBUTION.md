# Music credits

The three additional tracks below were downloaded from their creators' own
OpenGameArt posts on 2026-11-09. Each post identifies its track as
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Artist credit is retained, including the credit requested by The Cynic Project.

The game copies use Ogg Vorbis at 44.1 kHz. Stream timestamps were normalized,
and a constant gain adjustment makes them quiet background music; the full
recordings are retained without cuts or changes to tempo. Source and game-file SHA-256 hashes, exact download URLs,
durations and gain adjustments are recorded in `licensed_tracks.json`.
